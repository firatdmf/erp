The numeric attributes are written with stringformat, NOT floatformat: floatformat localizes, so with Turkish active a reserved 13.00 m came out as "13,00" and every Number() over it was NaN. That is what made a package header say "NaN m". What is DISPLAYED still goes through floatformat — a Turkish reader should read a Turkish number. Callers pass: r (the reservation), and optionally oiid (the order line the roll sits under), packid (the package it is in) and drag.{% endcomment %}
