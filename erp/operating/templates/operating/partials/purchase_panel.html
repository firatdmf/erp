The form is the purchase page itself, in a frame (?embed=1) — one purchase form, with its own scripts, rather than a second copy inside the order's. Saving posts in the background and closes the panel; closing by hand asks first when something was typed in it. Open with njOpenPurchasePanel(url). Included once per page. When saved, the page's njPurchaseSaved(data) takes the result in place if it has one (the order's edit form); otherwise the page reloads. {% endcomment %}
